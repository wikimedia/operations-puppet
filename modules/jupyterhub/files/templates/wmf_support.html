Need help?

First, look at the troubleshooting guide on Wikitech. If that does not solve the problem:

  • For urgent or blocking issues, ask in #talk-to-data-engineering on Slack. The Ops Week engineer will respond during business hours.
  • For other issues, create a Phabricator task for the Data-Platform-SRE team.

Please include the name of the stat host and the error text from this page.
